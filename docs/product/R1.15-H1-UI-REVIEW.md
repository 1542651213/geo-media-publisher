# R1.15-H.1 purple UI Candidate review

**UI Candidate only. Owner visual approval is required. Main remains the R1.15-H Candidate baseline.**

## Design principles

Deep purple-gray navigation and a calm white/light gray-purple canvas establish the brand. Purple marks actions and selection; ordinary reading text and borders remain neutral. Success, warnings and errors retain green, orange and red. Gradients are limited to the small brand mark. No page transitions, glow or glass effects.

The home page prioritizes human attention, generation problems and original publish-result reconciliation, followed by review and today's plan. It does not invent a publish-failure count from the generation-failure metric. Company context remains visible in the shell.

## Design system

[Tokens](../../apps/desktop/src/renderer/design/tokens.css) cover colors, typography, spacing, radius, elevation, focus, motion and layers. Primary `#6D5BD0`, hover `#5E4CC3`, soft selection `#F0EDFF`, sidebar `#221B3A`, canvas `#F6F7FB`, text `#2A2F45` and secondary `#656C83`. Semantic text shades are darker than decorative green/orange/red to maintain contrast.

Shared PageHeader, StatusBadge, EmptyState, LoadingState, StatCard and PublishWorkflow reduce repetition. Shared controls distinguish primary, secondary, tertiary and danger actions. Body text is 14px, compact/table/form text 13px, status and metadata generally 12px. The remaining 10–11px text is limited to small sidebar/build metadata. Primary, secondary, metadata, brand and semantic text colors pass the normal-text AA test on white, canvas and soft selection surfaces. Metadata was deepened to `#626A80` after independent review.

Dialogs preserve their existing close handlers and draft flush behavior. Focus stays inside visible dialogs, Escape invokes an existing close control, composition events are respected, and focus returns after closing. Reduced-motion preferences suppress the subtle skeleton/hover effects.
